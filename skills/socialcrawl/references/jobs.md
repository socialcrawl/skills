# Jobs

11 endpoints, all GET. Base URL `https://www.socialcrawl.dev`, auth header `x-api-key: $SOCIALCRAWL_API_KEY`.

**Credit costs:** 3 standard (1 credit), 4 advanced (5), 4 premium (10) - the exact cost is in each endpoint heading below.

Also LinkedIn organization-id resolution (feed the ids back into the LinkedIn search filters) and salary ranges by job title and country with title suggestions. Search endpoints are premium (10cr); detail is advanced (5cr); the salary and organization lookups are 1cr.
